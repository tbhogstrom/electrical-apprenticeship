## Source material

A hand-drawn wiring diagram came with the house, photographed and saved
at `photos/2026-07-31-original-wiring-diagram.PNG`. A first-pass,
**unverified** transcription of its numbered legend is in
`circuit-inventory.md`. Once the panel itself is physically surveyed
(breaker sizes, positions, condition), reconcile the panel's actual
breaker numbering against this diagram's numbering — they may not match
if the panel's been touched since the diagram was made.

A digitized, editable draft of the diagram (rooms + circuit markers +
legend) is at `diagrams/wiring-diagram-digitized.drawio` (open with the
free [draw.io](https://www.diagrams.net/) desktop app — already
installed via `brew install --cask drawio`), with a quick-view PNG at
`diagrams/wiring-diagram-digitized.png`. **Room positions in this
digitized version are approximate, not traced to scale** — treat it as a
starting point to correct against the original photo, not a survey-grade
floor plan.
