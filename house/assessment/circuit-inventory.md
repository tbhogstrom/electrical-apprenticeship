## Source

Transcribed from a hand-drawn wiring diagram that came with the house
(photographed and saved at
`photos/2026-07-31-original-wiring-diagram.PNG`). This diagram shows a
floor plan with ~20 numbered circuit markers and a numbered legend on the
right edge mapping numbers to rooms/loads.

**This table is a DRAFT, not verified.** Several legend entries were
only partially legible in the photo (small handwriting, phone camera
angle), and old house wiring diagrams commonly go stale after remodels,
so numbers may not match what's actually in the panel today.
**Do not treat any entry here as confirmed dead/live for safety
purposes** — every circuit still needs to be physically traced and
verified (breaker off + outlet tester / continuity check) as part of the
real assessment, per `panel-schedule.md` and `code-gap-survey.md`.

Re-photograph the diagram straight-on and in better light if you want a
more complete/accurate transcription of items 10 onward and the
partially-illegible entries above.
