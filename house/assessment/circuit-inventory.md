## Source

Transcribed from a hand-drawn wiring diagram that came with the house
(photographed and saved at
`photos/2026-07-31-original-wiring-diagram.PNG`; digitized floor-plan
trace at `diagrams/wiring-diagram-digitized.drawio`). The house has
three bedrooms (BR#1, BR#2, BR#3), a living room, kitchen, bath (with
exhaust fan), and a small washer/dryer closet, connected by a hallway.
The diagram shows ~20 numbered circuit markers and a numbered legend on
the right edge mapping numbers to rooms/loads, plus a few inline labels
written directly next to specific symbols (disposal, dishwasher).

**This table is a DRAFT, not verified**, even where marked "higher
confidence" — that phrase means the photo evidence was clearer for that
entry, not that it's been physically confirmed. Old house wiring diagrams
commonly go stale after remodels, so numbers may not match what's
actually in the panel today. **Do not treat any entry here as confirmed
dead/live for safety purposes** — every circuit still needs to be
physically traced and verified (breaker off + outlet tester /
continuity check) as part of the real assessment, per
`panel-schedule.md` and `code-gap-survey.md`.

There's also an unlabeled triangle symbol near BR#2/BR#3 with a
handwritten "?" next to it on the original — even the person who drew it
wasn't sure what it was. Worth asking whoever drew the diagram (if
known) rather than guessing.

Re-photograph the diagram straight-on and in better light if you want a
complete transcription of the legend entries above 9 (13, 14, 15, 17,
18, 19, 20 all appear on the diagram but their text descriptions were
off-frame or illegible).
